A weekly Letter about your own patterns

Each day, you answer a short check-in on your phone. Each week, you receive a Letter about the shape of your week, drawn from your own check-ins.

GLIOS is not medical, not therapy and not advice. It notices shapes that recur in what you report. It does not diagnose, judge or tell you what to do.

Each Letter is written by an AI, from your own check-ins and notes. Before you join, you'll see which AI service writes it and where your words go.

Cohort 1 is free. You can stop at any time.

What it won't do

No streaks. No scores. No badges. Nothing here is designed to hook you.

Miss a day and nothing chases you.

A pattern is only something that repeats. Checking in honestly, day after day, is what makes yours yours.

Who it's for

Adults who live in South Africa, can check in once a day on a phone, and understand that GLIOS is not a health service.

GLIOS is not a crisis service. If you're going through a mental-health crisis right now, it isn't the right support for you at the moment — SADAG answers 24 hours a day, toll-free, on 0800 567 567.
